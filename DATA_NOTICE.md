# Data attribution and scope

Source: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), **Payroll Based Journal Daily Nurse Staffing**, reporting periods **2024Q2–2026Q1**. Data were retrieved on September 13, 2026. The exact requests, retrieval timestamps, schema versions and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in [data/manifest.json](data/manifest.json).

The original raw data are not distributed in this repository. CMS-derived daily records, features, facility descriptors, model artifacts, predictions, statistical reports and dashboard screenshots are distributed for review and reproducibility. They describe public facility-level staffing; they are not patient records.

CMS remains the source of the underlying public data. Any code license added to this project must be scoped separately and must not be represented as a license of CMS material or as CMS endorsement. This publication does not assign a new license to third-party data. Consult the [official dataset](https://data.cms.gov/quality-of-care/payroll-based-journal-daily-nurse-staffing) and [source documentation](docs/SOURCES.md) for source terms and limitations.

This is an independent portfolio project. Publication and attribution do not imply CMS or employer endorsement.
